Welcome

A considered stay, two minutes from the water

Victoria Cottage sits within easy reach of Mudeford Quay, tucked into a quiet corner of the Dorset coast where the light seems to last longer and the pace slows without trying. It has been put together with the same care you'd expect from a small boutique hotel — nothing loud, nothing left to chance.

Inside, soft neutrals, natural materials and generous light give every room a settled, unhurried feel — a place designed to be lived in slowly, whether you're here for a long weekend or a longer escape.

Mudeford, Dorset

A quiet stretch of the Dorset coast

Mudeford Quay and its famously colourful beach huts sit a short stroll from the cottage, with Hengistbury Head and its sweep of sand and heathland just beyond. The harbour is lively without being loud — small boats, local seafood, and views out toward the Isle of Wight on a clear day.

Christchurch's historic priory and quay are a few minutes further on, and the wider Dorset and New Forest coastline opens up from there — ideal for slow exploring rather than ticking off a list.
